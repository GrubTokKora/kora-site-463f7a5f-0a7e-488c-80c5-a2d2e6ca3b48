Our story

Fine Italian, reimagined

Welcome to Antonio's Italian Bistro, where tradition meets innovation in a sophisticated and enchanting dining experience. Nestled in the heart of Darien, CT, our restaurant embodies the essence of fine Italian cuisine, reimagined with a modern twist.

From pristine white tablecloths to impeccable service, every detail is meticulously crafted to transport you into a world of culinary delight.

The experience

Dining is more than a meal

As you step into our elegant and inviting space, you'll be greeted by the soft glow of candlelight, the melodious strains of Italian music, and a warm, attentive staff ready to guide you through an unforgettable evening.

Each course is a journey through Italy's diverse culinary regions—whether you're savoring delicate handmade pasta, our signature chicken scarp, or fresh branzino.
