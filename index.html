Our Story

Where Tradition
Meets Innovation

Nestled along Boston Post Road in the heart of Darien, Connecticut, Antonio's Italian Bistro is a celebration of everything that makes Italian cooking enduring — honest ingredients, time-honoured technique, and the warmth of a table shared with people you love.

"Cooking is an act of love. Every dish we send out carries that intention."

— Antonio's Italian Bistro, Darien, CT

We honour the classics while welcoming the new — drawing on the rich heritage of Italian cuisine and refining it with a modern sensibility that feels at home in Fairfield County's most discerning dining room. Come as a guest. Leave as family.
